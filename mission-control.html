Collision Prediction

Estimate close approaches and potential conjunction events.

The collision engine propagates orbits forward in time and computes miss distances between active spacecraft and tracked debris. Results are summarized as probabilistic risk bands.

Rendezvous Mode

Plan proximity operations and docking timelines.

Configure phasing orbits, approach corridors, and keep-out zones. Visual cues help explain relative motion and safety margins for students and mission planners alike.

Rendezvous Timeline (Conceptual)
  1. T-3 orbits: Phasing burns to align with target RAAN.
  2. T-1 orbit: Height adjustment to reduce relative motion.
  3. T-10 min: Final approach, corridor monitoring.
  4. T-0: Soft capture and docking.

Laser Ablation

Conceptual interface for directed energy debris mitigation.

Evaluate notional laser ablation campaigns to reduce debris cross-section or alter orbits. Emphasis is placed on explaining the physics and operational constraints, not on weaponization.

Estimated delta-v is minimal but sufficient to lower perigee over repeated passes. This is a conceptual educational model.

Deorbit Simulation

Visualize controlled re-entry and deorbit maneuvers.

Model drag augmentation, perigee-lowering burns, and decay timelines. The simulator provides simple graphics to show how objects re-enter the atmosphere safely over time.

Deorbit Timeline (Concept)
  • D-30 days: Drag sail deployment, perigee 350 km.
  • D-10 days: Rapid decay phase, increased plasma heating.
  • D-1 day: Controlled corridor monitored by ground stations.
  • D-0: Burn-up completion above populated areas.
